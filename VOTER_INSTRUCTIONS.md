# 🗳️ Voter Instruction Workflow: Step-by-Step Guide

Welcome to the **MSU CICS Electronic Voting System**. This guide will walk you through the entire process, from creating your account to casting your final vote.

---

## Phase 1: Registration & Account Approval
Before you can vote, you must have an authorized voter account.

1.  **Navigate to Registration**: Go to the Signup page (DIS or DCS department links provided by your election officers).
2.  **Fill in Your Details**:
    *   **Full Name**: Enter your name as it appears in official records.
    *   **Institutional ID**: Must be 9 digits and start with `202` (e.g., 202100123).
    *   **Institutional Email**: Use your official `@s.msumain.edu.ph` email address.
    *   **Year Level & Department**: Select your current year and ensure the department matches your course.
3.  **Set a Secure Password**: Choose a password you will remember.
4.  **Submit Application**: Click "Submit Application".
5.  **Wait for Approval**: Your account is now **Pending**. The System Administrator must verify your details before you can log in. You will receive an update once approved.

---

## Phase 2: Accessing the System
Once your account is approved, you are ready to participate in active elections.

1.  **Login**: Use your **Institutional ID** and the **Password** you created during registration.
2.  **Dashboard**: Upon logging in, you will be directed to your **Voter Dashboard**.
3.  **Check Election Status**: 
    *   If an election is active, you will see the **Official Ballot**.
    *   If the election is locked or has not started, a "Pending" notice will be displayed.

---

## Phase 3: The Voting Process (Official Ballot)
The ballot is organized by position (e.g., President, Vice President, etc.).

1.  **Browse Candidates**: Scroll through each position to see the names, photos, and partylists of the candidates.
2.  **Select Your Choice**: Click on a candidate's card to select them.
    *   A **check mark** and a **navy blue highlight** indicate a selection.
    *   To change your mind, simply click the candidate again to deselect them.
3.  **Multi-Vote Positions**: Some positions (like Board Members) allow you to vote for more than one candidate.
    *   The system will show you how many you have selected (e.g., `2/4 selected`).
    *   You cannot select more than the allowed maximum.
4.  **Track Your Progress**: A floating bar at the bottom shows how many positions you have filled.

---

## Phase 4: Review & Final Submission
This is the most critical step. Once submitted, your vote **cannot be changed**.

1.  **Review Ballot**: Click the **"Review Ballot"** button.
    *   **Abstain Reminder**: If you have left any positions blank, a reminder will appear asking if you are sure you want to abstain. You can choose to "Go Back" to select candidates or "Proceed to Review".
2.  **Verify Selections**: A summary of your choices will appear in the Review Modal.
    *   **Green Check**: You have selected a candidate.
    *   **Red X (Abstain)**: Confirms the position is left blank.
3.  **Edit if Necessary**: If you want to change anything, click **"Edit Selections"** to go back to the ballot.
4.  **Cast Final Vote**: If everything is correct, click **"Cast Final Vote"**.

---

## Phase 5: Completion
1.  **Success Confirmation**: You will see a "Vote Successfully Cast!" message with a green shield icon.
2.  **Security**: Your ballot is now encrypted and recorded in the system. Your participation is complete.

---

## 💡 Tips for a Smooth Experience
*   **Stable Connection**: Ensure you have a stable internet connection before clicking "Cast Final Vote".
*   **One-Time Voting**: The system only allows you to vote **once** per election.
*   **Confidentiality**: Your vote is private. Do not share your password with anyone.
